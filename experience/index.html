Experience

The thread through my work is getting learned systems to behave well on real machines: on the water, under it, in the air and on a robot arm. I like problems where the model is only half the answer and the other half is making it hold up under noise, delay and limited hardware.

Work and research

LAT Aerospace

Aug 2026 – present
Computer Vision Engineer

I build the eyes of a drone's gimbal camera: detecting and tracking targets in visible and thermal video, and keeping the picture steady, all live on a small embedded GPU. Most of the work is about what happens when a good model meets a moving camera and a tight compute budget. I made a large transformer tracker half as deep and several times faster without losing accuracy, and learned that on embedded hardware, memory and data movement matter as much as the network itself.

MEERA Lab, Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi

Jun – Jul 2026
Research Intern, with Dr. Giulia De Masi

I studied how a team of underwater vehicles can search the seabed together when their sonar is unreliable and their acoustic links drop most messages. I trained the team with multi-agent reinforcement learning so the vehicles divide the area on their own and share what they find when they come within range. The idea I'm proudest of is letting the learned policy notice its own uncertainty and ask a classical planner for help only then, which keeps the planner's quality at a fraction of its cost.

Mowito Automations

Dec 2025 – Jan 2026
AI Intern

I worked on teaching a robot arm to route wire harnesses for car assembly, a long task with a floppy, unpredictable object. I collected demonstrations, adapted Vision-Language-Action models (π0.5 and SmolVLA) and compared them with ACT. Seeing where these large pretrained models succeed and where they lose the thread led me to split the task into short learned skills under a simple planner, and it is what drew me towards world models.

MAV Lab, IIT Madras

Apr 2024 – May 2026
Undergraduate researcher, with Dr. Abhilash Somayajula

My first research home. I used multi-agent reinforcement learning to teach ships with different dynamics to avoid each other in busy waters, and found that the agents could discover rule-following behaviour on their own. I also worked on pursuit and evasion, where an evading ship learns to escape several pursuers. It is where I learned to build my own simulators and to be careful about what a reward actually teaches.

Pravahan Engineering

May – Jul 2025
Software and Controls Intern

I modelled the dynamics of a hydrofoil ferry and designed the closed-loop control that keeps it lifted and stable at cruise, a reminder that good classical control is still the foundation under everything learned.

Leading Team Aritra

From 2024 to 2026 I led Team Aritra, IIT Madras's autonomous surface vessel team, through international competitions including Njord in Norway, VRX and RoboBoat. Beyond the boat itself, I kept our partnership with the Royal Norwegian Embassy going and enjoyed showing school students what marine robotics can do.
