Real-time EO/IR perception for UAV gimbals

LAT Aerospace · Aug 2026 – present

Detection, tracking and stabilization of electro-optical and thermal video, running live on a Jetson Orin NX on the drone.

  • Multi-object tracker for very small aerial targets (4–30 px) that holds tracks through camera motion and occlusion.
  • Improved a transformer single-object tracker under occlusion, then distilled it to half depth and deployed it in TensorRT FP16.
  • CUDA video stabilizer that removes shake and keeps intended gimbal pans.
  • Moving-target detection from a moving camera, EO–IR registration and target geolocation.

Multi-agent RL for underwater target search

MEERA Lab, Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi · with Dr. Giulia De Masi · Jun – Jul 2026

Five autonomous underwater vehicles search the seabed together, with noisy sonar and acoustic links that drop most messages.

  • GPU-batched multi-agent simulator in VMAS, and a shared MAPPO policy trained with a centralised critic and run decentrally.
  • Vehicles share belief maps when in range, so the team keeps working under heavy packet loss.
  • An entropy trigger hands uncertain decisions to a classical information-gain planner, at a fraction of its compute.

Vision-Language-Action models for wire harness routing

Mowito Automations · Dec 2025 – Jan 2026

Robot routing of wire harnesses for automotive assembly, a long task with a deformable object.

  • Collected teleoperated demonstrations on a LeRobot SO-101 arm and built language-annotated datasets per sub-task.
  • Compared π0.5 and SmolVLA against an ACT baseline; fine-tuned π0.5 with LoRA and ran it with asynchronous inference.
  • Hierarchical planning that splits routing into short learned motion primitives.
