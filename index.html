DWG NO. 00 — BRIEF

Field of operation

I build the decision layer for machines that have to navigate uncertainty — underwater, in the air, and on the water. My work sits at the intersection of control theory and multi-agent reinforcement learning: teaching fleets of autonomous vehicles to search, avoid collisions, and hold formation when sensing and communication can't be trusted.

Currently I'm a research intern at Sorbonne University's MEERA Lab, modelling belief-fusion strategies for underwater vehicles searching with degraded acoustic sensing. Earlier, I placed 1st and 3rd internationally in DRDO's Swarm Drone Rescue Challenge, took 2nd overall at Formula Bharat as a second-year EV team, and led Team Aritra's autonomous surface vessel programme through NTNU's Njord Challenge.

DWG NO. 01 — PUBLICATION

IEEE OCEANS 2026 · SANYA, CHINA · MAY 25–28

FUSED-Net: Fast Underwater Super-Resolution & Enhancement Distillation Network

A lightweight, real-time super-resolution model distilled from a high-fidelity GAN teacher — built to run enhancement on underwater imagery without the usual inference cost. Presented at the IEEE Oceanic Engineering Society's flagship marine-technology conference.

  • U-Net image enhancement to remove hue and occlusion, raising UIQM score
  • Super-resolution GAN generating 4× upscaled, high-frequency detail from low-res input
  • Knowledge distillation into a lightweight autoencoder student
  • Result: inference cut from 400ms to 30ms, model size reduced 50× with reconstruction accuracy maintained
