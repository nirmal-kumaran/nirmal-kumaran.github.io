About

I'm a robotics and computer vision engineer. I work on how robots perceive and act: robot learning, reinforcement learning, Vision-Language-Action models and world models, and making these systems run in real time on real hardware.

At LAT Aerospace I build detection, tracking and video stabilization for UAV gimbal cameras on embedded GPUs. Before this, I worked on multi-agent reinforcement learning for underwater vehicles with Dr. Giulia De Masi at Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi, and on Vision-Language-Action models for robot manipulation at Mowito Automations. I graduated in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Madras in 2026.

Feel free to read my CV or get in touch.
