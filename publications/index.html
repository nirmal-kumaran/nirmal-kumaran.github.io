FUSED-Net

FUSED-Net: Fast Underwater Super-Resolution and Enhancement Distillation Network

Nirmal Kumaran (first author) · IEEE OCEANS 2026, Sanya

A light, real-time model for underwater images. A U-Net removes colour cast and haze, a GAN upscales by 4×, and a small student network learns from the GAN so the result runs fast: inference drops from 400 ms to 30 ms with a 50× smaller model.

Multi-agent reinforcement learning and entropy-triggered planning for decentralised multi-AUV target search

Nirmal Kumaran, Giulia De Masi · journal paper in preparation

A shared MAPPO policy for teams of underwater vehicles with unreliable sonar and acoustic links, and a trigger that calls a classical planner only on uncertain decisions.
